Finding a property

Use the property search to search by location, select a property type and status, and set minimum or maximum yearly rent. Select Search to apply the filters. Open a property card to review its details.

Why can’t I see all property details?

Some information may be reserved for Premium accounts. The listing should explain which features are available on your plan. You can review the subscription section on the homepage for the current price and included features.

How much does Premium cost?

The current page lists Premium at ₦10,000 per month. Check the live subscription screen before paying in case pricing or features have changed.

How do I subscribe?

Sign in, choose the Premium plan and follow the payment instructions. Access should be enabled after the payment is verified. Keep your transaction reference if you need support.

Payment succeeded but my account is still Basic

Wait briefly and refresh or sign in again. If the plan still has not updated, email support with your account email, transaction reference, amount and approximate payment time. Do not send your password or full card details.

How do I stay safe?

Independently verify the owner or agent, property availability, rent, fees and ownership. View the property where possible and avoid paying before you are confident the listing and recipient are legitimate. Be careful when meeting someone you first contacted online.

How do I get help with my account?

Email support with the email address on your account and a short description of the issue. Never send your password, one-time codes or full payment-card details.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ocupant (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles personal information when you use our website and housing features. We intend to handle information fairly and transparently and to follow applicable Nigerian data-protection requirements.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account information: name, email address, login credentials in protected form, account type and account-related dates.
  • Listing and search information: property details you submit, search terms and filters, saved preferences and interactions with listings.
  • Communications: messages or information you send to support or through roommate/property features.
  • Payment status: subscription plan, transaction reference, amount, status and dates received from our payment provider. Payment-card details are handled by the provider, not intended to be stored by Ocupant.
  • Technical information: basic device/browser information, IP/log information and security events where collected by our hosting or service tools.
  • Location: approximate or precise location only when you provide it or enable a relevant browser feature, such as directions from your location.

2. Why we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, show property listings, apply plan access, process and verify subscriptions, provide roommate or property features, respond to support requests, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain the service, and meet legal obligations. We will not use personal information for a materially different purpose without an appropriate basis or notice.

3. Legal basis and choices

Depending on the activity and applicable law, processing may be needed to provide the service you request, comply with legal obligations, protect legitimate platform and user interests, or rely on your consent. You can choose not to provide some information, but certain features may then be unavailable. You can deny browser location permission and still use other features.

4. Sharing

We may share necessary information with hosting, database, analytics/security or payment service providers; with other users where required for a feature you choose to use; and with authorities or other parties when legally required or necessary to protect rights and safety. We do not sell personal data to advertisers. Third-party services process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

5. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, account operation, dispute handling, security and legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the data type and circumstances. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, object to or request restriction of certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and raise a complaint with the relevant data-protection authority. To make a request, email mrrichpoto@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

7. Children

Ocupant is intended for people legally able to use housing and rental services. Do not knowingly create an account or submit personal information for a child without lawful authority.

8. International processing and third-party links

Service providers may process information in locations outside Nigeria. Where applicable, we will take steps required by law for such transfers. Links to third-party websites are governed by their own privacy practices.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy when our practices or legal obligations change. The effective date above indicates the latest version date. Privacy questions and requests: mrrichpoto@gmail.com or +234 903 851 0864.

Please verify that this policy accurately reflects the production site, logs, analytics, hosting, payment-provider settings and actual retention practices. It is a practical draft, not legal advice.
